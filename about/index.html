About Propert-Ease

Why societies trust us with their governance

Propert-Ease was born from a simple frustration — watching managing committees drown in spreadsheets, WhatsApp chaos, and missed compliance deadlines. We set out to build a platform that makes society governance transparent, accountable, and effortless.

  • Purpose-built for Indian RWAs and Cooperative Housing Societies (CGHS)
  • Full compliance coverage — RCS Section 79, DCS Act, and RERA DLP tracking
  • Multi-tenant architecture with complete data isolation per society

Our Responsibility

We take ownership of your society's digital transformation. From onboarding to daily operations, we ensure every committee member and resident has the tools they need to govern effectively and transparently.

Our Approach

We collaborate closely with society committees to understand their unique governance challenges. Our platform is shaped by real-world feedback from Secretaries, Presidents, and Treasurers managing societies across India.

Our Mission

To digitize governance and compliance for every residential society in India — replacing manual ledgers, scattered documents, and opaque processes with a single, auditable, legally defensible digital platform.
